Bitcoin rebounds toward $93K as Fed cut bets firm but ETF outflows cap upside
BTC is up around 2% near $92.5K–93K after briefly surging to $94K ahead of Wednesday’s widely expected 25 bp Fed rate cut, helped by a softer dollar and steadier risk sentiment. However, Reuters and Bloomberg note that thin liquidity, April/October tariff-driven selloffs, and roughly $5 billion in recent U.S. spot ETF outflows are keeping the 2025 ‘rollercoaster’ on track to potentially end the year with its first annual loss since 2022.
Ethereum outperforms on upgrade and positioning tailwinds
ETH is up about 6% near $3,320, handily beating BTC as traders position for the upcoming Fusaka upgrade and see ether as a higher‐beta play on any post‐Fed relief rally. CoinDesk highlights aggressive whale long positioning and renewed buying from crypto investment firms such as BitMine, which recently added 97,000 ETH despite unrealized losses.
XRP lags broader move despite regulatory win momentum
XRP trades near $2.10, up a modest 1%–1.5% on the day and underperforming the broader altcoin complex. CoinDesk reports that while Ripple continues to notch regulatory and licensing progress in hubs like Singapore, today’s move came on below‐average volumes, raising questions about the durability of the latest bounce.
Solana and high‐beta alts track ETH-led risk-on shift
Solana is up roughly 3%–5% around $138–141, extending a week of gains as falling bond yields and expectations of easier Fed policy revive interest in high‐beta layer‐1s. On TradingEconomics and Kitco data, Solana, Cardano, Polkadot and Avalanche are all posting mid‐single to low‐double‐digit 24‐hour gains, reflecting broader rotation back into riskier tokens after last week’s BOJ‐linked selloff.
Macro backdrop: ‘hawkish cut’ risk keeps crypto tethered to equities and rates
Across Reuters, Yahoo Finance and Schwab, analysts emphasize that crypto is trading increasingly like a high‐beta equity index, with 2025 BTC–S&P and BTC–Nasdaq correlations around 0.5. Markets have nearly fully priced a 25 bp Fed cut, but a hawkish tone on 2026 could keep 10‐year yields near 4.2% and limit further upside for BTC and ETH in the very near term.
